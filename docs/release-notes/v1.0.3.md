# wflow 1.0.3: Editor canvas polish

Three small editor fixes spotted while recording demo footage. Cut as
a patch so the cleanups land for anyone who installed 1.0.2 last
week.

## Trigger bind moved to the top-right

The pinned chord-bind card lived in the top-left corner of the
canvas, sharing the left edge with the StepPalette. At short heights
the palette (vertically centered) climbed under the card. On hover
the palette expanded from 56 to 200 pixels wide and overlapped the
card entirely.

Moving the card to the top-right means the left edge belongs to the
palette and the right belongs to the trigger. They never collide
regardless of viewport size.

## Bind-a-chord dialog centers on the window

When the trigger card moved to the top-right, clicking it opened the
`ChordCaptureDialog` roughly centered on that small card and the
dialog spilled off the window's right edge. The dialog had
`anchors.centerIn: parent` and was inheriting whichever item it was
declared inside.

Setting `parent: Overlay.overlay` on the dialog component makes it
center on the window's overlay layer regardless of where it's
instantiated. The TriggersPage instance still positions correctly
because it was already declared inside a full-window Item.

## Tidy-smart column-wrap wires are visible again

The wire connecting the last card of one column to the first card of
the next was being drawn vertically: out the top of the source, up
over the column's other cards, into the bottom of the target. The
wire ran underneath two intermediate cards and you could barely see
it.

The axis picker had three cases (same-row, different-rows,
overlapping) and the "different-rows" branch fired for any target
not in the same row, which lumped diagonal column-wraps in with
same-column targets. Splitting that branch so true diagonals (no
overlap on either axis) pick horizontal lets the bezier arc cleanly
across the column gap, with both endpoints on the side edges of the
cards. Same-column targets still go vertical; truly stacked cards
still pick the larger centre-delta.
